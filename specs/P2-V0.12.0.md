# Parallax 2.0 v0.12.0 — Ambient Chroma Flow

Status: APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION
Spec ID: P2-V0.12.0
Depends on: P2-V0.11.1
Method: reference-informed ambient motion refinement + exact-head release verification

## 1. Objective

Refine the Parallax living background so it carries the slow, diffused, liquid color motion of the operator-provided visual reference while remaining unmistakably Parallax: darker, calmer, violet/indigo-led, readable, accessible, and subordinate to conversation.

## 2. User outcome

The workspace should feel continuously alive without looking like discrete animated blobs. Large color regions drift through one another like light diffusing through liquid glass. Purple, indigo, blue, and restrained magenta dominate; sparse warm amber/peach energy appears only as a secondary counterpoint. Motion remains slow enough to disappear from conscious attention while reading.

## 3. Scope

- Replace the current discrete lava-mass composition in `LivingSurface` with a broad diffused chroma field inspired by the supplied reference motion language.
- Preserve the dark Editorial Optical base and current conversation-material hierarchy.
- Use overlapping low-frequency color wells and warped haze rather than outlined or visibly bounded blobs.
- Keep violet, indigo, midnight blue, lavender, and restrained cyan as the primary Parallax chroma family.
- Permit sparse muted magenta and warm amber/peach blooms as secondary counterpoints, never dominant fields.
- Keep the central reading region darker than peripheral chroma.
- Preserve the active optical response etching effect unchanged.
- Preserve reduced-motion behavior by freezing time-dependent motion without removing the visual depth of the field.
- Preserve reduced-graphics fallback behavior.
- Preserve mobile, tablet, and desktop geometry established through v0.11.1.

## 4. Security

This release is visual-only and does not change the v0.10+ identity, authorization, session, API, persistence, Work Specification, Reason, Code, or execution-authority boundaries.

- Google/Supabase remains the identity provider boundary.
- Parallax server-owned authorization remains authoritative.
- No provider token, root bearer, service secret, or private credential is added to UI or visual state.
- The new background accepts only local visual uniforms (`resolution`, `time`, `energy`) and performs no network or storage operations.
- Existing signed HttpOnly session behavior and protected browser requests remain unchanged.

## 5. Ambient motion contract

The production background must satisfy all of the following:

- motion is low-frequency and continuous, with no obvious short loop, snap, bounce, particle burst, or directional sweep;
- color regions are broad and feathered, with transitions that read as diffused light rather than discrete shapes;
- the dominant palette remains dark indigo/violet/blue;
- magenta is restrained and warm amber/peach occupies only a small fraction of the visible field at any one time;
- cyan remains an optical accent rather than a full-field color;
- the center reading zone remains materially darker than the perimeter so text and translucent conversation material retain contrast;
- active Reason energy may modestly increase chroma intensity but must not materially increase speed;
- fine grain may remain at very low amplitude to prevent a flat digital gradient;
- the effect must remain full-viewport without horizontal overflow or layout influence.

## 6. Accessibility and performance

- `prefers-reduced-motion` / platform reduced-motion freezes time-dependent animation while preserving the composed color field.
- Reduced-graphics mode continues to provide functional hierarchy without requiring Skia.
- The shader must remain bounded to a single full-screen Skia runtime effect; do not introduce DOM particle systems, video playback, remote assets, or per-frame React state updates.
- Conversation text contrast and interaction targets remain unchanged.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

### AC-01 Diffused field character
The production `LivingSurface` uses broad feathered chroma fields and does not render hard or deliberately separated lava-blob edges.

### AC-02 Parallax palette hierarchy
Dark indigo/violet/blue remains dominant. Magenta and warm amber/peach are secondary and visually restrained. Cyan remains an accent.

### AC-03 Calm motion
Automated browser evidence confirms the Skia frame changes over time while the implementation uses low-frequency time scaling and does not alter interaction state or layout.

### AC-04 Reading-zone protection
The center of the workplane remains intentionally darker than the animated perimeter field.

### AC-05 Reduced motion
Reduced-motion freezes time-dependent background motion while preserving a coherent static field.

### AC-06 Conversation regression protection
The v0.11 conversation materials, theme-colored optical text etching, composer, Work Specification presentation, and mobile viewport fixes remain intact.

### AC-07 Product regression protection
Google identity/authorization, Reason, Code, SSE, durable conversations, Work Specifications, approved-spec execution binding, Engineering Runs, API protection, persistence, reduced graphics, and existing browser acceptance continue to pass.

### AC-08 Responsive integrity
Mobile, tablet, and desktop acceptance report no document/composer horizontal clipping and no regression to account/mode/Work Specification geometry.

### AC-09 Release verification
Specification validation, API tests, client typecheck/state tests, web export, browser/Skia acceptance, Google-auth browser acceptance, Code-binding browser acceptance, protected evaluation, DSPy release compilation, Vercel READY state, health/readiness, protected-route checks, and runtime-error checks pass before v0.12.0 is recorded as deployment-verified.

## 8. Non-goals

- Reproducing, embedding, or shipping the reference video.
- Copying its watermark, exact frames, or exact color sequence.
- Adding particles, stars, bubbles, lens flares, or fast visual effects.
- Changing conversation boxes, typography, navigation, authentication, authorization, API, database, or execution behavior.
- Replacing the theme-colored laser/optical text etching effect.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

- **Background becomes distracting:** use broad low-contrast fields, slow time scaling, and central darkening.
- **Warm color drifts off-brand:** cap amber/peach contribution and keep violet/indigo dominant.
- **Animation becomes expensive:** retain one Skia runtime effect with uniforms only; no React frame loop.
- **Reference becomes imitation rather than inspiration:** implement the motion principles only and use Parallax-specific palette, contrast, and reading-zone behavior.
- **Mobile contrast degrades:** preserve dark center protection and validate at 390x844 alongside tablet and desktop.
