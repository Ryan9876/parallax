## 1. Objective

Establish the Wave 6 outcome-routing layer that can choose among already-admissible engineering execution strategies using trustworthy quality, cost, elapsed-time, retry, reassignment and intervention evidence without allowing economic optimization to weaken correctness, safety, privacy, protected validation, independent evaluation or human-control boundaries.

S4 consumes exact provenance-bound evidence from accepted S1 agent protocol, S2 team orchestration and S3 independent evaluation plus server-owned routing policy. It first determines whether a strategy is admissible under exact identity, authority, capability, dependency and quality constraints. Only after those non-negotiable gates pass may S4 compare economic outcomes such as observed cost, duration, retries or intervention burden. Unknown, unavailable, stale, contradictory or low-confidence evidence must remain explicit and cannot be converted into fabricated performance history.

S4 produces immutable routing-decision evidence. It does not invoke providers, grant capabilities or credentials, mutate source, accept lineage, advance protected Engineering Run state, choose an S5 candidate winner, merge, deploy, complete REVIEW or bypass `HUMAN_REQUIRED`.

## 2. Fixed decisions

- **Correctness and governance precede economics.** Incompatible, unauthorized, deterministically failed, policy-rejected, not-independent or otherwise inadmissible strategies are excluded before cost/time comparison. A cheaper or faster option cannot purchase admission.
- **Protected deterministic validation remains authoritative.** S3 `SUPPORTED` evidence is necessary where policy requires it but never overrides deterministic validation. S4 cannot reinterpret deterministic failure as an economic tradeoff.
- **Only admitted evidence is trustworthy.** Agent/model/repository prose, self-reported cost, claimed latency, claimed success rate or unbound telemetry is untrusted until represented through an accepted bounded evidence contract with exact provenance.
- **Unknown is not zero.** Missing cost, duration, token, retry, quality or intervention evidence remains `UNKNOWN`/`UNAVAILABLE`; it cannot be scored as free, fast or successful.
- **Routing policy is server-owned.** Eligibility rules, quality floors, evidence freshness, economic weights, exploration bounds, fallback behavior, tie-breakers and policy identity/version/digest are immutable bounded server-owned inputs.
- **Observed evidence outranks estimates.** Where estimates are permitted, provenance must distinguish observed, derived/estimated, unavailable and unknown. Policy decides whether estimates may contribute and with what bounded confidence/penalty.
- **Project-private evidence stays private.** Cross-Project routing evidence is inadmissible unless a separate existing generalized-memory mechanism has already sanitized and admitted it. S4 does not create cross-Project authority.
- **Routing decisions are replayable.** The same exact routing context, strategy set, evidence set and policy version produces the same decision/fingerprint. Policy or evidence drift creates a new distinguishable decision.
- **Safe fallback is explicit.** Insufficient or contradictory evidence chooses a deterministic conservative fallback or `HUMAN_REQUIRED` according to server-owned policy. S4 never fabricates certainty simply to produce a selection.
- **Exploration is bounded and non-authoritative.** If policy permits exploration among admissible strategies, exploration limits are explicit and cannot create providers, capabilities, spending authority, source authority or validation bypass.
- **S4 produces evidence, not execution authority.** A routing result may recommend/select an already-admissible strategy identifier for an upstream protected orchestrator to consider. It contains no invocation credential or mutation method.
- **S5 remains distinct.** S4 may rank execution strategies using outcomes; it does not generate competing implementation candidates, synthesize them, or accept a winner.

## 3. Architecture requirements

### 3.1 Exact routing context

Define an immutable `RoutingContext`-equivalent contract containing at minimum:

- canonical Project ID;
- canonical Engineering Run ID;
- exact Work Specification ID, revision and digest;
- exact stable acceptance IDs in canonical order;
- accepted S1 protocol version / required agent evidence identity where applicable;
- accepted S2 orchestration identity/digest or equivalent exact team-plan evidence where applicable;
- accepted S3 evaluator-policy/evaluation identity required by the strategy quality contract;
- exact S4 routing-policy ID, version and digest;
- bounded decision sequence/operation identity where needed for replay provenance.

Construction fails closed on malformed UUID/digest/version values, duplicate/empty acceptance IDs, Project/run/spec mismatch, dependency-protocol drift or unsafe/unbounded text.

### 3.2 Admissible strategy contract

Define a bounded immutable `DevelopmentStrategy`-equivalent projection for an execution strategy already possible under accepted S1/S2 infrastructure. A strategy may describe:

- stable strategy ID/version;
- one-agent or bounded team-plan identity;
- exact admitted agent/team identity digests;
- work/coordination profile identifiers;
- required server capabilities as descriptive prerequisites only;
- optional model/provider class labels as non-authoritative routing evidence;
- policy-owned maximum exploration/attempt class where relevant.

A strategy cannot carry raw provider targets, credentials, arbitrary URLs, shell commands, source mutations, unregistered capabilities or self-granted authority.

### 3.3 Typed development-outcome evidence

Define a bounded `StrategyOutcomeEvidence`-equivalent record bound to the exact routing context and strategy identity. It may contain typed metrics for:

- S3 quality outcome and exact evaluation-record digest;
- deterministic protected-validation status/reference digest;
- elapsed runtime/duration;
- provider/request/token/cost observations where S1 provenance supports them;
- retry, recovery, reassignment and replan counts;
- human intervention / `HUMAN_REQUIRED` occurrence;
- completion/terminal disposition evidence;
- bounded failure-class or reliability observations.

Every metric carries provenance and state equivalent to `OBSERVED`, `ESTIMATED` where explicitly allowed, `UNAVAILABLE`, `UNKNOWN`, `STALE` or `INVALID`. Numeric values are finite and bounded. Missing values remain missing.

### 3.4 Evidence provenance, freshness and admission

Provide deterministic evidence admission that checks at minimum:

- exact Project/run/spec/acceptance binding;
- exact strategy identity;
- exact S1/S2/S3 dependency identity where the evidence depends on those layers;
- evidence source/provenance class;
- evidence digest/reference integrity;
- server-owned freshness policy;
- duplicate/conflicting observation handling;
- cross-Project privacy;
- bounded metric/value/schema validity.

Agent/model/repository text cannot promote a self-reported metric into an observed fact. Conflicting trusted observations fail closed or are preserved as contradiction evidence according to server-owned policy.

### 3.5 Server-owned routing policy

Define an immutable bounded `RoutingPolicy`-equivalent contract with:

- policy ID/version/content digest;
- permitted strategy classes;
- mandatory eligibility constraints;
- required deterministic-validation and S3 quality states;
- minimum quality/confidence/support floors where applicable;
- evidence freshness windows or sequence rules;
- allowed provenance classes per metric;
- bounded metric normalization rules;
- explicit quality/cost/time/retry/intervention weighting or lexicographic ordering;
- missing-evidence penalties/fallback behavior;
- exploration limits;
- deterministic tie-breaker;
- HUMAN_REQUIRED escalation rules.

Policy cannot add/remove Work Specification acceptance IDs, lower protected validation/evaluator policy, create capabilities or provider authority, or transform unavailable evidence into observed evidence.

### 3.6 Eligibility before optimization

Provide a deterministic eligibility phase that excludes any strategy with one or more of:

- Project/run/spec/acceptance mismatch;
- S1/S2 identity or policy drift;
- capability/authorization incompatibility;
- deterministic validation failure or missing mandatory protected evidence;
- S3 `DETERMINISTIC_BLOCKED`, `NOT_INDEPENDENT`, `POLICY_REJECTED`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` when policy requires sufficient quality evidence, or unresolved `HUMAN_REQUIRED`;
- stale/invalid mandatory telemetry;
- disallowed strategy/provider/model class under server-owned policy.

Eligibility output must expose bounded reason codes. Economic scoring never runs on an ineligible option as though it were merely expensive.

### 3.7 Bounded economic normalization and scoring

For eligible strategies only, normalize permitted metrics through deterministic bounded server-owned functions. At minimum:

- lower cost cannot compensate for violating a quality floor;
- lower duration cannot compensate for deterministic failure or unresolved human boundary;
- retry/reassignment/intervention burden remains explicit rather than hidden inside a single opaque score;
- unknown/unavailable metrics receive explicit policy treatment, never implicit zero;
- estimates, if allowed, cannot outrank better-proven observed evidence solely because the estimate is numerically favorable;
- all arithmetic rejects NaN/infinity/negative values where nonsensical and remains bounded against overflow/unbounded precision.

A total score may be used only after mandatory floors and eligibility checks. The decision record must retain the component values/provenance sufficient to explain the selection without hidden reasoning.

### 3.8 Deterministic selection and safe fallback

Define final bounded dispositions equivalent to:

- `SELECTED` — exactly one admissible strategy selected by policy;
- `FALLBACK_SELECTED` — policy-selected conservative admissible fallback because preferred economic evidence was insufficient;
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` — no safe automated comparison is possible;
- `NO_ADMISSIBLE_STRATEGY` — every strategy failed non-economic eligibility;
- `HUMAN_REQUIRED` — policy requires operator resolution;
- `POLICY_REJECTED` — routing input violates server-owned policy.

Ties use an explicit deterministic server-owned tie-breaker such as stable strategy identity, never arrival order or model preference. A decision can select only a strategy present in the admitted input set.

### 3.9 Bounded exploration

If the routing policy permits trying an under-observed but admissible strategy, define explicit maximum exploration count/budget class and evidence requirements. Exploration is optional and must fail closed when budget/attempt bounds are exhausted.

S4 itself does not issue provider calls or spend money. Its output may indicate an admissible exploration strategy, but the existing protected orchestration/provider layers remain responsible for actual authorization and execution.

### 3.10 Replay, idempotency and conflict detection

Define a deterministic routing fingerprint from exact routing context, ordered strategy identities, admitted outcome-evidence digests and routing-policy identity/version/digest.

Exact replay returns duplicate-safe equivalent decision evidence. Changed policy, strategy roster, evaluation record or admitted outcome evidence produces a distinguishable fingerprint. Competing incompatible decision records for one fingerprint fail closed rather than silently overwriting one another.

### 3.11 Safe downstream evidence contract

Provide deterministic safe serialization for S5/S6 containing only:

- exact canonical IDs/digests required for provenance;
- policy ID/version/digest;
- strategy IDs and eligibility dispositions/reason codes;
- bounded admitted metric observations with provenance/state;
- bounded normalized components/score when policy permits;
- selected/fallback strategy ID if any;
- explicit missing/contradictory evidence markers;
- routing fingerprint and decision-record digest.

Serialization excludes credentials, raw provider payloads, prompts, hidden reasoning, unrestricted logs, arbitrary URLs/commands and cross-Project private evidence. It exposes `grants_capabilities=false`, `invokes_provider=false`, `routes_spending=false`, `accepts_source_lineage=false`, `transitions_engineering_run=false`, `performs_merge=false`, `performs_deployment=false`, `completes_review=false` or equivalent structural guarantees.

### 3.12 Composition with accepted S1-S3

S4 implementation must reuse/import accepted S1-S3 contracts rather than duplicating or weakening them. If implementation reveals that S4 requires a semantic change to S1 agent evidence, S2 orchestration identity/admission, S3 evaluation policy/outcome, protected validation, source lineage, provider authorization or Engineering Run state, stop and expand #267 scope through Control Tower before changing that shared contract.

## 6. Security and privacy

All repository, source, agent, evaluator and model content is untrusted evidence. S4 may consume only canonical bounded S1-S3 and protected evidence. Text cannot redefine eligibility, policy, quality floors, cost values, provider scope, capabilities or authority.

Economic optimization is a potential pressure toward unsafe shortcuts, so precedence is structural: eligibility and correctness are computed before economic score. No score function receives an ineligible strategy as a viable competitor.

Cost/time telemetry must not expose API keys, billing credentials, raw provider responses, headers/cookies/environment, private prompts or hidden reasoning. Provider/model labels are bounded non-secret identifiers only. Project-private outcome histories remain Project-private unless an already-authorized generalized-memory mechanism supplies a sanitized admitted reference.

Numeric evidence rejects NaN, infinity, impossible negative counts/cost/duration and unbounded magnitude. Unknown/unavailable evidence cannot be coerced to zero. Policy-controlled estimates are marked explicitly and cannot masquerade as provider-observed values.

Routing output contains no execution token, provider credential, arbitrary destination, raw command or source patch. Selecting a strategy is evidence for a protected upstream orchestrator, not permission to execute it.

`HUMAN_REQUIRED` remains a protected boundary. S4 may emit or preserve it but cannot resolve or bypass it itself.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

### AC-01 Exact canonical routing identity
Every routing decision binds canonical Project ID, Engineering Run ID, exact Work Specification ID/revision/digest, stable acceptance IDs, accepted S1/S2/S3 dependency identity where applicable and exact routing-policy ID/version/digest. Malformed, duplicate, empty, stale or mismatched identity fails closed.

### AC-02 Trustworthy telemetry provenance and missing-data semantics
Every quality/cost/time/retry/reassignment/intervention/completion signal is bounded and provenance-labeled. Tests prove agent/model/repository self-report cannot become `OBSERVED` authority, unknown/unavailable values remain explicit rather than zero, and invalid/stale/cross-Project evidence is rejected or preserved as bounded non-admissible evidence.

### AC-03 Governance and correctness precede economics
Eligibility excludes unauthorized/incompatible strategies and any strategy failing mandatory protected deterministic/S3 quality gates before economic scoring. Tests prove a cheaper/faster candidate cannot outrank or re-enter after deterministic failure, S3 policy rejection/not-independence or unresolved mandatory HUMAN_REQUIRED.

### AC-04 Server-owned immutable routing policy
Routing binds exact policy ID/version/digest covering eligibility, metric provenance, freshness, floors, normalization/weights or ordering, missing-evidence treatment, exploration bounds, fallback and tie-breaking. Agent/source/model content cannot change policy or acceptance criteria.

### AC-05 Bounded deterministic economic comparison
Only eligible strategies are compared. Numeric normalization is finite/bounded and quality floors remain non-tradeable. Cost/time/retry/intervention components remain inspectable. Unknown and estimates follow explicit policy rather than favorable implicit defaults.

### AC-06 Deterministic selection, tie-breaking and fallback
The same exact admitted input produces the same disposition/strategy/fingerprint. Ties use server-owned deterministic identity rules. Insufficient/contradictory evidence produces explicit fallback, insufficient evidence or HUMAN_REQUIRED according to policy rather than fabricated certainty.

### AC-07 Replay/idempotency and conflict detection
Exact replay is duplicate-safe. Evidence, strategy-set or policy drift yields a new fingerprint. Competing incompatible records for one fingerprint fail closed and no replay duplicates execution/provider mutation because S4 owns no such mutation.

### AC-08 Bounded exploration without authority expansion
If enabled, exploration can select only already-admissible strategies and is bounded by explicit policy limits. Tests prove exploration cannot register providers, grant capabilities, create credentials, invoke spending, mutate source or bypass validation/review.

### AC-09 Cross-Project privacy and safe serialization
Project-private telemetry cannot cross Project boundaries without an already-admitted sanitized/generalized reference. Safe output excludes secrets, raw provider payloads/prompts, hidden reasoning, arbitrary URLs/commands and unrestricted logs while retaining enough bounded provenance to explain the decision.

### AC-10 Evidence-only downstream authority
Routing records structurally expose no source-lineage acceptance, protected Engineering Run transition, provider invocation, spending, capability grant, merge/deployment, approval, REVIEW completion or candidate-winner authority. S5/S6 consume S4 only as bounded routing evidence.

### AC-11 Accepted S1-S3 and protected regressions remain intact
Focused S4 tests plus relevant S1 agent protocol, S2 team orchestration, S3 independent evaluation, protected validation/evaluation and bounded-autonomy regression suites remain green. S4 does not duplicate or weaken their identity/admission/authority semantics.

Temporary DSPy workflow changes are restored byte-for-byte before runtime implementation/final candidate.
