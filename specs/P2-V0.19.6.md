## 1. Objective

Create the permanent integrated Wave 6 reference proof demonstrating that Parallax can take an approved development objective and, without requiring the operator to manually manage agents, deterministically select an appropriate governed engineering team, coordinate dependency-safe work, recover/reassign bounded failures, independently evaluate exact candidates, use trustworthy quality/cost/time evidence, selectively compete or synthesize alternatives when policy admits it, and publish an exact-lineage working Preview that stops at `REVIEW` / `HUMAN_REQUIRED`.

S6 is an integration-proof and permanent benchmark layer. It composes accepted S1-S5 contracts and the existing protected Engineering Run, source-lineage, BUILD/TEST/VERIFY, Preview publication and REVIEW boundaries. It does not create a second orchestration path, evaluator, routing authority, competition authority, canonical source writer, provider credential path, release path or approval path.

The proof must be replay-safe, provenance-complete, privacy-safe and suitable for exact-head Control Tower release judgment. It must include positive, recovery and adversarial fixtures and a trustworthy comparison against the accepted Wave 5 baseline on appropriate cases.

## 2. Fixed decisions

- **S6 proves composition; it does not invent new S1-S5 authority.** Any semantic gap in an accepted upstream contract is routed back through Control Tower rather than patched around in the benchmark.
- **Canonical identity is continuous end to end.** Project, Work Specification revision/digest, stable acceptance IDs, Engineering Run, task/attempt, candidate/source-lineage, evaluation, routing/competition and Preview evidence remain exact and provenance-bound.
- **Deterministic validation remains authoritative.** Agent/evaluator/routing/competition judgment cannot outvote failed or missing protected BUILD/TEST/VERIFY evidence.
- **The operator does not manually manage agents.** Server-owned policy decides one-agent versus bounded multi-agent formation, dependencies, reassignment and selective competition within existing S1-S5 limits.
- **Failure recovery is durable and bounded.** Recovery/reassignment must derive from accepted worker/lease/checkpoint/evidence state; replay cannot duplicate mutation or publication.
- **Independent evaluation is structurally separate from implementer self-assessment.** Producer self-evaluation cannot satisfy S3 independence.
- **Economics informs but does not purchase correctness.** Quality/cost/time/retry/intervention evidence is provenance-bound; unknown or stale evidence remains explicit and cannot be coerced to zero cost or high confidence.
- **Competition remains selective.** S5 may compare or request synthesis only when its admitted server-owned policy justifies it; S6 cannot force tournament behavior for benchmark convenience.
- **Synthesis is fresh lineage.** A synthesized implementation must receive new attempt/candidate/lineage identity and fresh protected validation plus fresh independent evaluation.
- **Preview is the autonomous publication ceiling.** Exact-lineage Preview publication may occur through the existing protected path; production deployment, merge authority, approval and REVIEW completion remain outside S6.
- **`REVIEW` / `HUMAN_REQUIRED` is preserved.** The reference proof must stop there and prove that adversarial agent/model content cannot bypass it.
- **No benchmark-only production behavior.** Fixtures may inject bounded test evidence through existing public/internal contracts but cannot add code paths that exist only to make the benchmark pass.
- **No hidden reasoning persistence.** Permanent proof evidence contains bounded reasons, digests and measured facts, not chain-of-thought, raw prompts, unrestricted logs or provider payloads.

## 3. Architecture requirements

### 3.1 Integrated reference-case identity

Define a bounded immutable `AgenticReferenceCase`-equivalent contract containing at minimum:

- stable reference-case ID/version;
- canonical Project ID;
- exact Work Specification ID/revision/digest and stable acceptance IDs;
- exact Engineering Run ID;
- bounded objective/task graph identity;
- expected one-agent or multi-agent policy class where the fixture requires a specific structural property;
- exact accepted S1-S5 policy/version/digest identities relevant to the case;
- expected protected terminal ceiling (`REVIEW` or `HUMAN_REQUIRED`);
- exact baseline-comparison class when the case participates in Wave 5 comparison.

Malformed, duplicate, stale, cross-Project or mismatched identity fails closed before execution.

### 3.2 Permanent benchmark fixture set

Provide permanent deterministic fixtures covering at minimum:

1. a single-agent task where the smallest adequate team is one compatible agent;
2. dependency-safe multi-agent parallel work with non-overlapping coordination domains;
3. overlapping work that must serialize rather than race;
4. durable worker failure followed by policy-valid recovery/reassignment;
5. independent evaluation where producer self-assessment is rejected as non-independent;
6. economic routing where quality floors remain authoritative and known/unknown cost/time evidence is explicit;
7. selective competition with a supported winner;
8. a no-competition case proving multiple available agents/candidates alone do not justify competition;
9. synthesis requiring a fresh candidate lineage, validation and independent evaluation;
10. exact-lineage Preview publication followed by `REVIEW` / `HUMAN_REQUIRED` stop;
11. replay/retry after process recreation without duplicate canonical mutation or duplicate Preview publication;
12. adversarial authority and privacy cases defined in §3.11.

Fixtures must remain bounded in size and deterministic in expected protected facts. They may use local/fake adapters only where those adapters implement the same accepted protocol and cannot bypass admission.

### 3.3 S1 agent evidence composition

Reference proof consumes S1 agent capability/task/result/checkpoint/evidence records exactly as admitted by the accepted S1 protocol.

The proof demonstrates:

- compatible capability selection only;
- exact task/attempt binding;
- stale/revoked/replay/competing-terminal rejection;
- no capability or credential creation by agent output;
- bounded result/evidence serialization;
- provider/adaptor interchangeability where the accepted reference adapters support it.

S6 may not add a second agent-result admission function.

### 3.4 S2 team orchestration composition

Use accepted S2 team planning/scheduling/reassignment contracts to demonstrate:

- smallest adequate team formation;
- deterministic dependency-safe ordering;
- permitted parallel execution only for independent coordination domains;
- conservative serialization of overlapping work;
- deterministic assignment identities and generation increments;
- durable reassignment only from accepted failure/recovery evidence;
- hard limits on team size, concurrency, retry, replan, reassignment and no-progress rounds.

No fixture may directly choose an otherwise-ineligible agent to satisfy an expected output.

### 3.5 Protected implementation and exact source-lineage continuity

The integrated proof must bind each admitted implementation candidate to the existing canonical Project/run/spec/acceptance and source-lineage contracts.

For any candidate considered successful:

- canonical source mutation occurs only through the existing protected single-writer path;
- exact content/source-lineage identity is preserved through BUILD/TEST/VERIFY;
- stale-parent/CAS and replay protections remain enforced;
- failure/retry cannot duplicate accepted canonical mutation;
- benchmark code never writes canonical lineage directly.

### 3.6 S3 independent evaluation composition

Use accepted S3 evaluation evidence only after protected deterministic validation state is available.

The proof must establish:

- evaluator identity is structurally independent from producer identity where policy requires independence;
- producer self-assessment cannot satisfy the requirement;
- deterministic failure/missing protected evidence yields the accepted blocked disposition regardless of evaluator preference;
- evaluation binds exact candidate/source-lineage and policy identity;
- replay is duplicate-safe and conflicting evaluation records fail closed.

### 3.7 S4 routing and development economics composition

Use accepted S4 routing/outcome evidence to prove policy can choose bounded next actions from trustworthy outcome facts while preserving protected quality floors.

Measured components must distinguish at least:

- quality/protected-validation state;
- elapsed-time state and provenance;
- cost/usage state and provenance;
- retry/reassignment/intervention counts;
- unknown/missing/stale evidence.

Unknown values stay unknown. Lower cost or shorter duration cannot override failed correctness, unresolved `HUMAN_REQUIRED` or required evaluation support.

### 3.8 S5 selective competition and synthesis composition

Use accepted S5 contracts to demonstrate both competitive and non-competitive paths.

The proof must show:

- competition triggers only from policy-admitted evidence;
- only eligible exact candidates enter comparison;
- deterministic validation and S3 independence precede economic comparison;
- supported winner identity is deterministic and evidence-only;
- S5 winner evidence does not itself accept canonical source;
- synthesis requests create a distinct downstream implementation attempt/candidate/lineage;
- parent validation/evaluation cannot be inherited by the synthesized candidate;
- fresh protected validation/evaluation is required before the synthesized candidate can re-enter comparison.

### 3.9 Exact-lineage Preview publication proof

Compose the existing protected delivery path to publish a Preview only from the exact accepted candidate/source lineage supported by the run.

The reference proof must bind:

- canonical Project/run/spec/acceptance identity;
- exact accepted candidate/source-lineage identity;
- required protected BUILD/TEST/VERIFY evidence;
- exact publication/provider action identity;
- resulting Preview reference/URL evidence in bounded safe form;
- replay/idempotency identity preventing duplicate publication on retry.

Preview proof cannot grant production-deployment authority or turn a Preview reference into canonical source acceptance by itself.

### 3.10 REVIEW/HUMAN_REQUIRED ceiling and operator handoff

The integrated flow ends in the existing protected `REVIEW` / `HUMAN_REQUIRED` state. S6 must prove:

- no agent/evaluator/routing/competition output can complete REVIEW;
- no benchmark fixture can invoke production deployment or merge as part of success;
- the safe evidence presented for operator judgment contains exact identities, protected outcomes, bounded quality/economic evidence and Preview reference without secrets or hidden reasoning;
- replay of the handoff does not create a second publication/mutation.

### 3.11 Adversarial and negative proof set

Permanent adversarial fixtures must prove rejection/fail-closed behavior for at minimum:

- agent attempts to grant itself a new capability or provider/tool authority;
- agent/model content requesting raw credentials or unrestricted shell/network access;
- acceptance-ID/spec redefinition inside agent/evaluator text;
- producer self-acceptance or producer self-evaluation used as independent quality proof;
- deterministic validation weakening or fabricated success evidence;
- cross-Project private source/evidence leakage;
- stale/duplicate/replayed/competing terminal agent results;
- stale source parent/CAS conflict;
- stale or cross-candidate evaluation/routing/competition evidence;
- lower-cost failed candidate attempting to beat a correct candidate;
- competition outside S5 policy bounds;
- synthesis attempting to inherit parent validation/evaluation;
- unauthorized canonical source acceptance, merge, production deployment or REVIEW completion.

### 3.12 Replay, recovery and process recreation

For selected positive/recovery cases, execute the same logical reference case across process recreation/retry boundaries using the existing durable worker/source/provider evidence contracts.

The proof must establish that exact replay or recovery:

- retains canonical Project/run/spec/source identity;
- resumes from durable evidence rather than in-memory convenience;
- does not duplicate accepted source mutation;
- does not duplicate provider publication/action when idempotency applies;
- increments generation/attempt identity only where accepted recovery policy requires it;
- remains bounded under repeated no-progress/failure conditions.

### 3.13 Wave 5 baseline comparison

Provide a permanent comparison harness for appropriate reference cases using the accepted Wave 5 generalized-delivery behavior as the baseline and Wave 6 integrated behavior as the challenger.

The comparison must use measured or explicitly fixture-bound facts and report at minimum:

- protected correctness/governance parity;
- completion reliability;
- operator/manual intervention count;
- quality/evaluation outcome where meaningful;
- elapsed time where trustworthy;
- cost/usage where trustworthy;
- retry/recovery behavior.

Wave 6 release evidence must demonstrate a material improvement in at least one meaningful dimension on appropriate cases without degrading correctness, safety, privacy or governance. Unknown or incomparable dimensions remain explicitly unknown/incomparable rather than being scored optimistically.

### 3.14 Reference-proof result and safe serialization

Define a bounded immutable `AgenticReferenceProofResult`-equivalent record containing only:

- reference-case ID/version;
- exact canonical Project/run/spec/acceptance identities/digests;
- exact relevant S1-S5 policy/evidence digests;
- team/assignment/recovery disposition summaries with bounded reason codes;
- exact candidate/source-lineage/protected-validation/evaluation/routing/competition identities;
- Preview publication identity/reference where applicable;
- final protected ceiling state;
- measured comparison components with provenance/known-state markers;
- replay/recovery evidence identities;
- overall bounded disposition such as `SUPPORTED`, `DETERMINISTIC_BLOCKED`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, `POLICY_REJECTED` or `HUMAN_REQUIRED`;
- proof fingerprint and result digest.

Serialization excludes source bytes, patches, secrets, credentials, raw provider payloads, prompts, chain-of-thought, unrestricted logs, arbitrary commands/URLs and cross-Project private evidence. It exposes equivalent guarantees that the proof itself grants no capabilities, provider/spending authority, canonical source acceptance, Engineering Run transition override, merge, production deployment, approval or REVIEW completion.

### 3.15 No duplicate authority and upstream-gap rule

S6 implementation must reuse accepted S1-S5 and existing protected runtime/delivery contracts by import/composition. If implementation requires changing accepted upstream semantics, protected source-lineage admission, provider authorization, deterministic validation, Engineering Run lifecycle or REVIEW authority, stop and return the gap to Control Tower before modifying that shared contract.

A benchmark helper may normalize fixtures/evidence but cannot become an alternate production authority path.

## 6. Security, privacy and reliability

All agent/model/repository/source/evaluator content is untrusted evidence. Only accepted bounded contracts may influence team formation, implementation admission, evaluation, routing, competition/synthesis, source acceptance or publication. Text cannot redefine acceptance criteria, quality floors, policy limits, provider scope or REVIEW authority.

Cross-Project source and evidence isolation is structural. Exact digests identify evidence; they do not grant access. Reference fixtures proving privacy failure use synthetic bounded evidence and cannot import another real Project's private data.

The reference harness may invoke only existing bounded test/reference adapters. It may not store raw credentials, create provider authority or loosen existing sandbox/network controls. Provider actions remain separately authorized and idempotency-bound.

Failure/recovery testing must exercise durable state rather than relying solely on in-memory mocks for claims about process recreation. Where a fixture substitutes a local durable test store, the proof must exercise the same accepted persistence/replay semantics and identify the substitution as test evidence.

Production deployment is not part of S6 worker success. No production mutation is required to prove the Wave 6 architecture.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

### AC-01 Exact end-to-end canonical identity continuity
Every supported reference case binds one canonical Project, exact Work Specification revision/digest and stable acceptance IDs, one Engineering Run, exact task/attempt identities, exact candidate/source-lineage identity and exact relevant S1-S5 policy/evidence identities through protected execution, evaluation, routing/competition and Preview evidence. Mismatch, drift, duplicate or cross-Project identity fails closed.

### AC-02 Autonomous smallest-adequate team formation and dependency-safe coordination
Permanent fixtures prove S2 independently chooses one versus bounded multi-agent teams from admitted capabilities, schedules dependency-safe parallel work, serializes overlapping coordination domains and does not require manual operator assignment. Ineligible agents cannot be forced into the team by fixture or agent content.

### AC-03 Durable bounded recovery and reassignment
A permanent failure/recovery fixture proves worker/agent failure is recovered or reassigned only from durable accepted evidence with correct generation/attempt identity, bounded retry/reassignment/no-progress behavior and no duplicate accepted source mutation after process recreation/retry.

### AC-04 Protected validation precedence and independent quality judgment
Permanent fixtures prove protected deterministic BUILD/TEST/VERIFY evidence remains authoritative, producer self-assessment cannot satisfy S3 independence, failed/missing deterministic evidence cannot be outvoted, and exact replay/conflicting evaluation behavior remains deterministic and fail-closed.

### AC-05 Trustworthy outcome routing and development economics
S4 composition exposes provenance-bound quality, elapsed-time, cost/usage, retry/reassignment and intervention evidence with explicit known/unknown/stale state. Economic advantage cannot override correctness, required evaluation support or unresolved `HUMAN_REQUIRED`.

### AC-06 Selective competition and fresh-lineage synthesis
S5 composition proves competition occurs only when policy admits it, non-competitive cases remain single-candidate, only eligible candidates enter deterministic comparison, winner evidence remains non-accepting, and synthesis creates a fresh candidate/attempt/lineage requiring fresh protected validation and independent evaluation.

### AC-07 Exact-lineage replay-safe Preview publication
A permanent integrated case proves the supported exact candidate/source lineage is the lineage delivered to Preview through the existing protected provider path, publication evidence is exact and bounded, and replay/process recreation does not duplicate publication or substitute a stale lineage.

### AC-08 REVIEW/HUMAN_REQUIRED autonomous ceiling
Successful integrated execution ends at the protected operator-review ceiling. Fixtures and adversarial cases prove no agent/evaluator/routing/competition/benchmark content can merge, production-deploy, approve, complete REVIEW or bypass `HUMAN_REQUIRED`.

### AC-09 Adversarial authority, validation and privacy resistance
Permanent negative fixtures prove capability/credential escalation, acceptance redefinition, self-acceptance, validation weakening/fabrication, cross-Project leakage, stale/replay/conflicting evidence, lower-cost failed-candidate preference, out-of-policy competition, inherited synthesis validation and unauthorized merge/deploy/REVIEW attempts are rejected or fail closed with bounded evidence.

### AC-10 Deterministic replay, recovery and safe proof serialization
Exact reference-case replay produces equivalent proof fingerprints/results; admitted identity/policy/evidence drift produces distinguishable fingerprints; conflicting records fail closed. Safe proof serialization excludes secrets/source bytes/raw payloads/prompts/hidden reasoning/arbitrary commands and grants no new authority.

### AC-11 Measured Wave 5 comparison without governance regression
For appropriate permanent cases, the harness reports trustworthy Wave 5 baseline versus Wave 6 challenger evidence and demonstrates material improvement in at least one of completion reliability, operator intervention, quality, cost or elapsed time while protected correctness, safety, privacy and governance do not regress. Unknown/incomparable dimensions remain explicit.

### AC-12 Exact-head release evidence and worker boundary
`P2-V0.19.6` has stable acceptance IDs and authentic DSPy SpecCritic + SpecCompiler evidence before semantic implementation; temporary DSPy workflow changes are restored byte-for-byte; focused S6 and relevant S1-S5/protected-runtime regressions pass; exact-head Workstream Spec Validation, Bounded Autonomy Pilot and P2 CI pass; PR targets `integration/wave6-agentic-control-plane`; worker stops `READY FOR INTEGRATION` with no merge, deployment, production mutation or release claim.
