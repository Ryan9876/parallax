## 1. Objective

Establish the Wave 6 candidate-competition layer that can selectively compare multiple implementation candidates when accepted evidence shows that the expected quality/reliability benefit justifies the bounded extra cost and elapsed time, while preserving deterministic validation, exact source-lineage identity, independent evaluation, privacy, replay safety and human-control boundaries.

S5 consumes accepted S2 orchestration evidence, S3 independent-evaluation evidence and S4 routing/economic evidence only as provenance-bound inputs. It may decide whether a bounded competition is warranted, compare already-produced admissible candidates, deterministically identify a supported winner, or request bounded synthesis when no existing candidate is sufficient and synthesis policy permits another attempt.

S5 does **not** mutate or accept canonical source lineage. A synthesis request describes evidence and parent candidate identities for an existing protected implementation path to produce a distinct new candidate. That synthesized candidate has a new exact lineage/attempt identity and must undergo fresh deterministic BUILD/TEST/VERIFY and fresh independent S3 evaluation before it can become eligible for comparison or later canonical acceptance.

S5 output is immutable competition evidence. It grants no provider/model/spending authority, credentials, filesystem/shell/network authority, source-lineage acceptance, protected Engineering Run transition, merge/deployment authority, approval, REVIEW completion or `HUMAN_REQUIRED` bypass.

## 2. Fixed decisions

- **Competition is selective, not default.** Server-owned policy decides whether competition is justified from exact task/routing/economic evidence. One admissible candidate remains sufficient when policy does not justify extra candidates.
- **Correctness precedes comparison.** Deterministic/protected validation is authoritative. A failed, stale, mismatched or incomplete candidate is disqualified before evaluator score, cost, speed or vote-like comparison.
- **Independent evaluation remains required where policy says so.** S3 evaluator evidence must bind the exact candidate and remain independent of its producer. Producer self-evaluation cannot become comparison authority.
- **S4 economics may justify competition but cannot purchase correctness.** Cost/time/retry/intervention evidence can determine whether added competition is worth attempting and can act as bounded tie/utility evidence only after protected eligibility is satisfied.
- **Candidate lineages remain isolated.** Candidate source/evidence identities are immutable. S5 never treats fragments from multiple lineages as a validated source package.
- **Synthesis is a new candidate, not a merge.** Synthesis creates a request for a separate protected implementation attempt. It cannot splice source directly into canonical lineage and cannot inherit parent validation/evaluation as if unchanged.
- **Fresh validation is mandatory after synthesis.** A synthesized candidate is ineligible until exact-lineage deterministic BUILD/TEST/VERIFY and independent evaluation required by policy have completed on that new lineage.
- **Competition policy is server-owned.** Candidate count, competition trigger, required evidence, quality floors, cost/time/attempt/synthesis limits, comparison order, tie-breaker, fallback and HUMAN_REQUIRED behavior are immutable bounded policy inputs.
- **No majority vote or model-confidence oracle.** Candidate selection is deterministic from admitted protected facts and policy. Agent/evaluator prose, arrival order and unsupported confidence cannot declare a winner.
- **Replay is deterministic.** The same exact context, candidate roster, policy and admitted evidence produce the same competition fingerprint and result.
- **Privacy is structural.** Project-private source/evidence cannot be imported from another Project to create, compare or synthesize candidates unless an existing separate generalized-memory authority has already sanitized and admitted a non-source reference.
- **S5 produces evidence, not canonical acceptance.** `WINNER_SUPPORTED` means an exact candidate is supported by the competition policy; it does not advance canonical source, Engineering Run state, merge/deploy or REVIEW.

## 3. Architecture requirements

### 3.1 Exact competition context

Define an immutable `CompetitionContext`-equivalent contract containing at minimum:

- canonical Project ID;
- canonical Engineering Run ID;
- exact Work Specification ID, revision and digest;
- exact stable acceptance IDs in canonical order;
- accepted S2 orchestration/team-policy identity required for candidate production provenance;
- accepted S3 evaluator-policy ID/version/digest;
- accepted S4 routing-policy/decision identity or exact routing-evidence digest when used to justify competition;
- exact S5 competition-policy ID/version/digest;
- bounded competition operation/sequence identity.

Construction fails closed on malformed UUID/digest/version/reference values, empty/duplicate acceptance IDs, dependency identity drift, Project/run/spec mismatch or unbounded text.

### 3.2 Candidate identity and provenance

Define a bounded immutable `CompetitionCandidate`-equivalent projection for each candidate containing at minimum:

- stable candidate ID/revision/attempt identity;
- exact candidate/source-lineage digest;
- exact producer agent/team identity digest(s);
- exact Project/run/spec/acceptance binding;
- exact protected deterministic-validation evidence digest/status;
- exact S3 evaluation-record digest and policy binding;
- optional exact S2 assignment/team-plan evidence digest;
- optional exact S4 outcome/routing evidence digest where economic evidence is used.

A candidate cannot carry raw source bytes, patches, shell commands, credentials, arbitrary URLs, provider tokens or self-granted capabilities inside the competition decision contract.

Duplicate candidate IDs with different lineage/evidence fail closed. The same lineage cannot masquerade as distinct alternatives by changing display identity alone.

### 3.3 Candidate eligibility before competition

Provide deterministic candidate admission that excludes any candidate with one or more of:

- Project/run/spec/acceptance mismatch;
- missing/mismatched/stale exact candidate/source-lineage identity;
- failed or missing mandatory protected deterministic validation;
- S3 evaluator-policy drift;
- evaluator identity not structurally independent from producer identity;
- S3 outcome other than policy-permitted support state;
- unresolved `HUMAN_REQUIRED`;
- cross-Project private evidence;
- malformed/duplicate/conflicting provenance;
- policy-disallowed candidate/producer class.

Eligibility emits bounded reason codes. Ineligible candidates never enter winner scoring or synthesis-parent selection as though they were merely lower-quality options.

### 3.4 Server-owned competition policy

Define an immutable bounded `CompetitionPolicy`-equivalent contract with:

- policy ID/version/content digest;
- maximum candidate count and minimum candidates for actual comparison;
- server-owned trigger rules for whether competition is justified;
- required protected validation and S3 evaluation states;
- minimum quality/confidence floors where applicable;
- allowed S4 economic/routing evidence classes and freshness rules;
- maximum extra cost/time/attempt/retry budget classes;
- maximum synthesis attempts and maximum synthesis parents;
- deterministic comparison order/weights or lexicographic rules;
- deterministic tie-breaker;
- fallback/no-selection/HUMAN_REQUIRED rules;
- maximum replay-safe no-progress rounds.

Policy cannot change Work Specification acceptance IDs, weaken deterministic validation/S3 evaluator policy, create capabilities/providers/credentials, authorize spending, or accept source lineage.

### 3.5 Selective competition trigger

Define a deterministic `should_compete`-equivalent decision using exact admitted inputs. Policy may justify competition from bounded evidence such as:

- multiple already-admissible candidates exist and material quality uncertainty remains;
- S4 indicates a bounded expected quality/reliability benefit that justifies added comparison cost;
- prior accepted outcome evidence shows policy-defined ambiguity/risk requiring an alternative;
- a protected task class is explicitly configured to require more than one candidate.

Competition is not justified merely because multiple agents are available. Missing economic/risk evidence follows explicit policy fallback rather than being interpreted as free competition.

The trigger returns bounded dispositions equivalent to `COMPETE`, `SINGLE_CANDIDATE_SUFFICIENT`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, `POLICY_REJECTED` or `HUMAN_REQUIRED`.

### 3.6 Deterministic candidate comparison

For eligible candidates only, define deterministic comparison with precedence equivalent to:

1. protected deterministic validation eligibility;
2. required S3 independent-evaluation support and policy floors;
3. acceptance/quality dimension coverage required by server policy;
4. policy-defined quality/reliability comparison;
5. permitted S4 cost/time/retry/intervention evidence only after non-economic floors pass;
6. deterministic stable candidate identity as final tie-breaker.

No lower cost, shorter duration, agent preference, evaluator prose length, arrival order or majority vote can compensate for deterministic failure or missing mandatory support.

The comparison record retains bounded component/reason evidence sufficient to explain the outcome without hidden reasoning.

### 3.7 Winner dispositions and evidence-only authority

Define bounded final dispositions equivalent to:

- `WINNER_SUPPORTED` — one exact eligible candidate is supported by deterministic competition policy;
- `SINGLE_CANDIDATE_SUFFICIENT` — policy determined extra competition was unnecessary and one already-admissible candidate remains supported;
- `SYNTHESIS_REQUESTED` — no existing candidate is sufficient and policy permits one bounded fresh synthesis attempt;
- `NO_ELIGIBLE_CANDIDATE` — no candidate passed protected eligibility;
- `NO_CLEAR_WINNER` — eligible candidates remain indistinguishable/insufficient under policy;
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` — required comparison/competition evidence is missing or stale;
- `POLICY_REJECTED` — inputs violate competition policy;
- `HUMAN_REQUIRED` — protected operator resolution is required.

`WINNER_SUPPORTED` and `SINGLE_CANDIDATE_SUFFICIENT` are evidence dispositions only. They cannot call source acceptance, transition Engineering Run state, invoke providers, merge/deploy or complete REVIEW.

### 3.8 Bounded synthesis request contract

When policy emits `SYNTHESIS_REQUESTED`, define a bounded immutable `SynthesisRequest`-equivalent record containing only:

- exact competition context/fingerprint;
- exact ordered parent candidate IDs and lineage/evaluation digests;
- bounded normalized defect/gap evidence references;
- exact target acceptance IDs requiring improvement;
- server-owned synthesis-policy identity/version/digest;
- new synthesis attempt/request identity;
- explicit maximum attempt/budget class;
- `fresh_validation_required=true` and `fresh_independent_evaluation_required=true`.

The request contains no raw source splice, patch, arbitrary command, credential, provider target or execution token. Existing protected S1/S2/runtime infrastructure must separately authorize and perform any implementation labor.

### 3.9 New-lineage synthesis semantics

Any implementation produced from a synthesis request is a distinct new candidate identity/attempt/lineage. Parent lineages remain immutable evidence references only.

The synthesized candidate cannot inherit parent protected validation/evaluation status. Before re-entering S5 comparison it must present fresh exact-lineage protected BUILD/TEST/VERIFY evidence and fresh S3 independent-evaluation evidence under the exact current policy.

A synthesis implementation may reuse only evidence or source continuity through existing protected source/runtime mechanisms. S5 itself owns no filesystem/source mutation method.

### 3.10 Competition bounds and no-progress behavior

Enforce hard server-owned bounds for at minimum:

- candidates per competition;
- extra candidate-generation attempts;
- synthesis attempts;
- synthesis parent count;
- total competition rounds;
- cost/time/attempt/retry class;
- no-progress repetitions;
- evidence references/diagnostic size.

Exhaustion returns explicit bounded fallback/no-winner/HUMAN_REQUIRED evidence. It cannot silently add agents/candidates, retry indefinitely, lower quality requirements or widen provider/spending authority.

### 3.11 Replay, idempotency and conflict detection

Define a deterministic competition fingerprint over exact context, ordered candidate identity/lineage/evaluation/routing evidence digests and competition-policy identity/version/digest.

Exact replay returns duplicate-safe equivalent decision evidence. Candidate roster, lineage, protected validation, S3 evaluation, S4 routing evidence or policy drift creates a distinguishable fingerprint. Competing incompatible records for one fingerprint fail closed.

A duplicate synthesis request cannot cause a second implementation/provider action because S5 owns no execution mutation and downstream protected orchestration must bind the exact request identity.

### 3.12 Safe downstream evidence contract

Provide deterministic safe serialization for S6 containing only:

- exact canonical Project/run/spec/acceptance IDs/digests;
- competition-policy ID/version/digest;
- exact candidate IDs/lineage/evaluation digests;
- eligibility/comparison dispositions and bounded reason codes;
- permitted bounded S4 economic components with provenance/state;
- selected supported candidate ID if any;
- synthesis request identity and parent IDs if applicable;
- competition fingerprint/record digest;
- explicit fresh-validation/evaluation requirements for synthesis.

Serialization excludes source bytes, patches, credentials, raw provider payloads, prompts, hidden reasoning, arbitrary URLs/commands, unrestricted logs and cross-Project private evidence. It structurally exposes `grants_capabilities=false`, `invokes_provider=false`, `routes_spending=false`, `accepts_source_lineage=false`, `transitions_engineering_run=false`, `performs_merge=false`, `performs_deployment=false`, `completes_review=false` or equivalent guarantees.

### 3.13 Composition with accepted S1-S4

S5 implementation must reuse/import accepted S2 orchestration, S3 independent evaluation and S4 outcome-routing/economic contracts rather than duplicating or weakening them. S1 remains the underlying exact agent task/result evidence protocol where candidate producer identity is required.

If implementation reveals that S5 requires a semantic change to accepted S1-S4 contracts, protected deterministic validation, source lineage, provider authorization, Engineering Run state or REVIEW policy, stop and expand #268 scope through Control Tower before changing that shared contract.

## 6. Security and privacy

All repository, source, agent, evaluator and model content is untrusted evidence. S5 consumes only exact bounded admitted evidence from accepted S1-S4 and protected validation/source-lineage systems. Content cannot redefine candidate eligibility, acceptance IDs, competition policy, evaluator independence, quality floors, budget bounds, provider scope or authority.

Competition creates incentive to multiply compute/cost, so candidate count, attempts, synthesis and economic budgets are hard server-owned bounds. S5 never issues provider requests or authorizes spending itself.

Candidate isolation is security-relevant. An exact source-lineage digest is evidence identity, not permission to read another Project's source. Cross-Project private source/evidence is rejected before comparison/synthesis-parent admission.

Synthesis inputs are bounded normalized evidence references, not raw source concatenation. A synthesis request cannot carry secrets, credentials, arbitrary URLs/commands, provider payloads or hidden reasoning. Any implementation labor occurs through existing protected agent/runtime boundaries.

A synthesized candidate must prove new exact-lineage validation and independent evaluation. Parent success cannot be copied to the new lineage. Deterministic validation remains authoritative over evaluator/comparison judgment.

`HUMAN_REQUIRED` remains a protected boundary. S5 may emit or preserve it but cannot resolve or bypass it.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

### AC-01 Exact canonical competition identity
Every competition decision binds canonical Project ID, Engineering Run ID, exact Work Specification ID/revision/digest, stable acceptance IDs, exact S2/S3/S4 dependency identity where applicable and exact competition-policy ID/version/digest. Malformed, duplicate, stale, mismatched or cross-Project identity fails closed.

### AC-02 Selective bounded competition trigger
Server-owned policy invokes competition only when admitted evidence justifies additional alternatives. Tests prove multiple available agents/candidates alone do not force competition, missing economic/risk evidence is not treated as free competition, and trigger dispositions are deterministic and bounded.

### AC-03 Exact candidate lineage isolation and admission
Each candidate is provenance-bound to one exact Project/run/spec/acceptance set, producer identity, candidate/source-lineage digest, protected-validation evidence and S3 evaluation evidence. Duplicate/mismatched/conflicting candidate identities fail closed and separate display IDs cannot make one lineage appear as independent alternatives.

### AC-04 Deterministic validation and independent evaluation precede comparison
Candidates failing mandatory deterministic validation or S3 independence/policy requirements are excluded before quality/economic comparison. Tests prove cheaper/faster or agent/evaluator-preferred failed candidates cannot re-enter or win.

### AC-05 Server-owned immutable competition policy
Competition binds exact policy ID/version/digest covering trigger rules, candidate/evidence requirements, quality floors, comparison order/weights, candidate/synthesis/cost/time/attempt/no-progress bounds, tie-breaking, fallback and HUMAN_REQUIRED behavior. Agent/source/model content cannot modify policy or acceptance criteria.

### AC-06 Deterministic winner comparison and tie-breaking
The same exact eligible candidate/evidence set produces the same comparison components, disposition, supported-candidate identity and fingerprint. Ties use explicit server-owned stable identity rules, never arrival order, majority vote or model preference.

### AC-07 S4 economics cannot override correctness
S4 routing/cost/time/retry/intervention evidence may influence whether competition is justified and permitted post-eligibility utility/ties only. Tests prove economic advantage cannot override protected failure, evaluator-policy failure, unresolved HUMAN_REQUIRED or quality floors.

### AC-08 Synthesis creates a new candidate lineage
A synthesis disposition emits only a bounded synthesis request bound to exact parent evidence and target acceptance gaps. The resulting implementation must have a new candidate/attempt/lineage identity and cannot inherit parent deterministic validation or S3 evaluation status.

### AC-09 Fresh validation/evaluation after synthesis
A synthesized candidate is ineligible until fresh exact-lineage protected BUILD/TEST/VERIFY and fresh policy-valid independent evaluation bind the new candidate. Tests prove stale/parent validation/evaluation evidence cannot satisfy the synthesized candidate.

### AC-10 Hard bounds, privacy and no-progress termination
Candidate count, competition rounds, extra attempts, synthesis attempts/parents, economic budget class, evidence size and no-progress behavior are bounded. Exhaustion terminates explicitly without unbounded agents/candidates, threshold weakening or cross-Project source/evidence access.

### AC-11 Replay/idempotency, safe serialization and evidence-only authority
Exact replay is duplicate-safe; candidate/policy/evidence drift produces a new fingerprint; conflicting records fail closed. Safe serialization excludes secrets/source bytes/raw provider payloads/prompts/hidden reasoning/arbitrary URLs/commands and grants no capability, provider/spending, source acceptance, Engineering Run transition, merge/deploy, approval or REVIEW authority.

### AC-12 Accepted S1-S4 regressions and exact-head worker boundary
Focused S5 tests plus relevant accepted S1 agent, S2 team, S3 evaluation, S4 routing/economic, source-lineage, protected validation/evaluation, provider and bounded-autonomy regressions remain green. `P2-V0.19.5` has stable acceptance IDs and authentic DSPy evidence before semantic implementation; temporary DSPy workflow changes are restored byte-for-byte; exact-head Workstream Spec Validation, Bounded Autonomy Pilot and P2 CI pass; PR targets the Wave 6 integration branch and worker stops `READY FOR INTEGRATION` with no merge/deploy.
